## 174 - 3.1
- [Profiles] New Profiles screen: bundle animation scales, screen width, battery presets and developer toggles into one profile and apply with a tap.
- [Notification widget] Home screen widget listing notifications matched by the new "Show on Widget" rule action.
- [Boost widget] One-tap widget that clears app caches and closes background apps, reporting freed storage and RAM; tiny, normal and wide layouts.
- [Per-app DPI] Set a DPI per app, applied automatically when the app comes to the foreground and reverted on exit; range limits, reset, clear all and a foreground service with Usage Access and Shizuku checks.
- [Always On Display] AOD Quick Settings tile.
- [Developer] Added "Disable rotation suggestions" and "Remove high volume warning" (applied again on boot).
- [Rules] Notification permissions are now requested based on the actions your rules actually use.
