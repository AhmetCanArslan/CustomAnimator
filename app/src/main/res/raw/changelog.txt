## 177 - 3.2
- Screenshot actions: copy or delete a new screenshot from a notification or a floating button, plus a "Copy screenshot" Quick Settings tile.
- Hotspot Manager: start or stop the hotspot, see and block connected devices, edit name, password, security, band and limits, share by QR, with a Quick Settings tile.
- Sound tile: a Quick Settings tile that opens the volume panel or the media output picker on press or long press.
- Game Mode: restricts background apps while your games run, with a Quick Settings tile and a home screen widget.
- Compilation mode: choose how apps are compiled (Everything, Speed, Speed profile, Verify).
- Per-app width: set a different smallest width for each app and apply it automatically while the app is in the foreground.
- Game Mode improvements: more reliable background-app tracking and improved notifications.
- Expanded terminal command suggestions and Arabic language support.
- Boost: a new System performance boost page to maximize your mobile performance.
